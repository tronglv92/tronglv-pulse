# Platform Engineering Rules

## Mission

Build internal platforms that enable developers to deliver software safely, quickly, and reliably.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Developer experience first  
- Self-service over tickets  
- Automation over manual processes  
- Standardization over customization  
- Security and reliability by default  

---

## Platform Design

- Provide reusable components  
- Abstract infrastructure complexity  
- Enable self-service workflows  
- Offer clear APIs and tooling  

---

## Infrastructure Standards

- Everything as Code  
- Immutable infrastructure  
- Versioned modules  
- Reproducible environments  
- Standardized templates  

---

## Developer Experience

- Simple onboarding  
- Clear documentation  
- CLI or portal access  
- Fast feedback loops  

---

## Reliability

- Built-in observability  
- Automated recovery  
- High availability  
- Capacity planning  

---

## Security

- Centralized identity management  
- Role-based access control  
- Secret management  
- Secure defaults  

---

## Automation

- Automated provisioning  
- Automated deployments  
- Automated compliance  
- Automated testing  

---

## Observability

- Logs, metrics, tracing as a platform feature  
- Standard alerting  
- Built-in dashboards  
- SLO-driven monitoring  

---

## Governance

- Standard templates  
- Policy as Code  
- Compliance automation  
- Audit trails  

---

## Lifecycle Management

- Automated upgrades  
- Dependency management  
- Deprecation policies  
- Clear ownership  

---

## Success Metrics

- Deployment frequency  
- Lead time for changes  
- Mean time to recovery  
- Change failure rate  
- Developer satisfaction
